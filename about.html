A little bit of everything

Life is rarely boring around here.

I'm a mom, a fur mom, a hobby farmer, a traveler, and a creative at heart. My days are usually a mix of family life, tending to our little collection of animals, dreaming up our next adventure, and finding something new to keep me busy.

I love the simple things just as much as the exciting ones — a slow morning at home, getting my hands dirty outside, exploring somewhere I've never been, or getting happily lost in a creative project.

There is almost always a project in progress, a trip being planned, or an animal reminding me that I'm definitely not in charge. And honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way.
