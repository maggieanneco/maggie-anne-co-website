How it works

A little structure. Lots of collaboration.

Clear steps, thoughtful details, and room to make the project feel unmistakably yours.

01

We chat.

We'll talk about your business, goals, audience, ideas, and what you need your website to do.

02

Make a plan.

03

Make it yours.

I'll build the design and we'll work through two consolidated rounds of revisions with your feedback.

04

Launch day.

After final approval and the $250 final payment, we'll prepare your site for production launch and handoff.

You'll know what stage we're in, what I need from you, and what's coming next. Project timing depends on receiving content, feedback, and approvals along the way.
